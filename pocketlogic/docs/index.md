# Pocket Logic Uploader

**Pocket Logic** is a YouTube Shorts channel that explains everyday money in under 40 seconds:
hidden fees, discounts, interest and the math behind prices.

**Pocket Logic Uploader** is a private automation tool operated by the channel owner. It produces
short educational videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the
YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

- Privacy policy: [privacy.md](privacy.md)
